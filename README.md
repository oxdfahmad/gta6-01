**Solmar Bay** — an original open-world crime game built for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. You are Leo Marin, a wheelman in the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar Bay, working five story missions for fixer Mara Solis against the harbour boss known as the Commodore. Explore a dense 3D city on foot and by car, steal parked cars, outrun police at wanted levels 0–5, fight gunmen, take taxi fares, race the beachfront time trial, hunt ten hidden packages worth $50 each, and save your progress. Everything — city, characters, vehicles, story, art, sound and music — is original; nothing is taken from Rockstar Games or any commercial game. Sound is synthesised live with WebAudio plus an original Solmar radio loop; the game runs entirely from static files with zero network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. Controls: WASD move/drive, mouse drag camera, click to shoot, E enter/exit car, Shift run, Space handbrake, T taxi job, R beach race, P pause, M mute. Progress saves to localStorage.
