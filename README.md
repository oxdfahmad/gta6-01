**Solmar Bay** is an original open-world crime game made for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. It stars Leo Marin, a wheelman working the docks of a sun-soaked coastal city, taking five story jobs from the fixer Mara Solis while the harbour boss known as the Commodore sends his crew after him.

What is in the game:

- A drivable city on foot and by car — steal parked rides or pull drivers out of traffic, with 28 traffic cars and 44 pedestrians on the streets.
- A wanted system from 0 to 5 stars: police cars chase and ram you, and officers on foot move in at 3+ stars. Get caught slow and it is BUSTED; run out of health and it is WASTED.
- Five chained story missions with beacons, dialogue and cash rewards, ending in a fight with the Commodore's guards and his armoured car.
- Pistol combat with gunmen, muzzle flash and tracers, plus side work: taxi fares, a 60-second beachfront time trial, and 10 hidden packages worth $50 each.
- A full HUD — cash, health, ammo, wanted stars, speed, clock, mission tracker and a live minimap — plus a title screen, pause, autosave to localStorage and a day-to-night clock.
- Sound and music synthesised live with WebAudio, over an original Solmar radio loop shipped as a local file.

Everything here — city, characters, vehicles, story, art, sound and music — is original work created for this bounty. Nothing comes from Rockstar Games or any commercial game, and the game runs from plain static files with zero network requests.

## Play it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open localhost:8080 in desktop Chrome. WASD moves and drives, mouse drag turns the camera, click shoots, E enters or exits a car, Shift runs, Space is the handbrake, T starts a taxi fare, R starts the beach race, P pauses, M mutes. Your run autosaves as you play.
